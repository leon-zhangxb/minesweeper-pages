Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a game?

On the home screen, tap any of the six difficulty cards, or the Daily Challenge card. The board is ready immediately. Your first tap is always safe — mines are placed after your first move, so you never lose on the opening click (the Daily Challenge is the only exception: its board is fixed for everyone, so the first tap carries the normal risk).

What difficulty levels are available?

Beginner (9×9, 10 mines), Intermediate (9×12, 20 mines), Expert (9×16, 30 mines), Ultra (12×21, 50 mines), Hell (16×30, 99 mines), Daily Challenge (10×16, 33 mines, one shared board per day), and Custom (9–36 columns by 9–64 rows, with any mine count).

What is the Daily Challenge?

Every day the app generates one board for all players worldwide from the day’s date — same layout, same mines, same seed. It resets at local midnight. Completing it marks the day as done, updates your streak, and submits your time to the daily Game Center leaderboard (when Game Center is available). Consecutive days feed the “Unbroken Streak” achievement, with checkpoints at 7, 30, and 100 days.

How does Custom difficulty work?

Choose any board width between 9 and 36 columns, any height between 9 and 64 rows, and any mine count from 1 up to (rows × columns) − 1. The configuration is saved to your device and applied the next time you play Custom.

How do I flag a cell I think is a mine?

Two ways: long-press a closed cell to drop or remove a flag, or tap the flag icon in the toolbar to enter “flag mode” and then tap cells normally to toggle flags. The mine counter at the top shows remaining mines minus flags.

What is chord tap?

When a revealed number is surrounded by exactly that many flags, tap the number again to auto-reveal all remaining unflagged neighbors. This is the classic “chord” shortcut from desktop Minesweeper.

What does the “Hint” button do?

It reveals one random safe cell. Use it when you are truly stuck — but note that hint reveals do count as normal moves and the timer keeps running.

How do I change the visual theme?

Tap Options on the top bar, choose Theme, and drag the slider between iOS, Candy, and Neon. The preview updates live as you drag.

How do I turn the music and sound effects on or off?

Tap Options, choose Sound, and toggle background music and game effects independently. Your choices are saved automatically.

Where are my best times stored?

Locally on your device, in iOS UserDefaults — one record per difficulty level, plus Daily Challenge history and your streak. If Game Center is enabled, completion times are also submitted to the corresponding leaderboards on Apple’s servers. Deleting the app removes local records; Game Center entries are managed by Apple.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. The game is fully playable offline. The only network use is Apple Game Center (leaderboards and achievements), which is optional and only active when you are signed in to Game Center on your device.

Do I need an account or login?

No registration is required. Signing in to Apple Game Center is optional — it only adds leaderboards and achievements. The app has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Where can I see leaderboards and achievements?

Tap Options → Game Center to open the Game Center dashboard, or use Apple’s Game Center app. Leaderboards cover each classic difficulty plus a daily board that resets every day.

How do I switch the interface language?

The app follows your system language and supports 16 languages. To change it, go to iOS Settings → General → Language & Region, or set a per-app language in iOS Settings → Minesweeper → Language.

What are the system requirements?

iOS 16.0 or later. The app supports iPhone and iPad, in both portrait and landscape orientation.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your device. All locally stored data (difficulty choice, custom configuration, best times, Daily Challenge records, theme and sound preferences) is removed together with the app. Game Center scores and achievements live on Apple’s servers and can be managed in iOS Settings → Game Center.

常见问题

如何开始一局游戏？

在首页点击任意一个难度卡片，或点击“每日挑战”卡片即可开局。棋盘会立刻就绪。 第一次点击始终是安全的——应用会在您首次落子后才布雷，因此您永远不会一开局就踩雷 （每日挑战是唯一例外：它的棋盘对全球玩家固定，首击存在正常风险）。

有哪些难度？

初级（9×9，10 雷）、中级（9×12，20 雷）、高级（9×16，30 雷）、 顶级（12×21，50 雷）、地狱（16×30，99 雷）、每日挑战（10×16，33 雷， 每天全球同题），以及自定义（9–36 列、9–64 行，地雷数自选）。

什么是每日挑战？

每天应用会根据当天日期生成一块全球同题棋盘——布局、雷位与种子完全一致， 本地时间 0 点重置。完成后会标记当日已挑战、更新连续天数，并把用时提交到每日 Game Center 排行榜（需可用的 Game Center）。连续挑战天数对应“坚持不懈”成就， 在 7 / 30 / 100 天三个档位解锁进度。

自定义难度如何使用？

可设置列数为 9–36，行数为 9–64，地雷数为 1 至（行×列−1） 之间的任意值。配置会保存到您的设备，下次选择“自定义”时自动生效。

如何给可疑的格子插旗？

两种方式：长按一个未翻开的格子可插旗 / 取消插旗；或先点击工具栏上的小旗图标 进入“插旗模式”，然后点击格子来切换插旗状态。顶部计数器显示 “剩余雷数 − 已插旗数”。

什么是“和弦点击”？

当一个已翻开的数字周围的旗数与该数字相等时，再次点击该数字会自动翻开其 周围所有未插旗的格子。这是桌面版扫雷经典的“和弦”快捷操作。

“灯泡提示”按钮有什么用？

会自动翻开一个随机的安全格。建议在实在卡住时使用——提示翻开的格子算正常 落子，计时器会继续。

如何更换视觉主题？

点击顶栏“选项”，选择“主题”，在 iOS、果冻、霓虹三种风格之间拖动滑块即可， 预览会实时变化。

如何开关背景音乐与音效？

点击“选项”，选择“音效”，可分别开关背景音乐与游戏音效， 选择会自动保存。

我的最佳成绩保存在哪里？

本地记录保存在您的设备 iOS UserDefaults 中——每个难度一条，另含每日挑战 历史与连续天数。若启用了 Game Center，通关用时还会提交到 Apple 服务器上的对应排行榜。 删除应用会清除本地记录；Game Center 上的成绩由 Apple 管理。

应用需要联网吗？

不需要。游戏可完全离线游玩。唯一的联网行为是 Apple Game Center（排行榜与成就）， 它是可选功能，仅在您于设备上登录 Game Center 时生效。

需要注册账号或登录吗？

无需注册。登录 Apple Game Center 为可选项——它只用于排行榜与成就。 本应用不含广告，也不含内购。

在哪里查看排行榜和成就？

点击“选项”→“Game Center”可打开 Game Center 面板， 也可使用 Apple 的 Game Center App。排行榜覆盖各经典难度，另有一个每日重置的每日榜。

如何切换界面语言？

本应用跟随系统语言，共支持 16 种语言。如需切换，请前往 iOS 设置 → 通用 → 语言与地区；也可在 iOS 设置 → 经典扫雷 → 语言中 单独设置本应用的语言。

系统要求是什么？

需要 iOS 16.0 或更高版本。支持 iPhone 与 iPad，支持竖屏与横屏。

如何删除我的数据？

从设备上删除本应用即可。所有本地存储的数据（难度选择、自定义配置、最佳成绩、 每日挑战记录、主题与音效偏好）会随应用一并移除。Game Center 上的成绩与成就在 Apple 服务器上，可在 iOS 设置 → Game Center 中管理。
