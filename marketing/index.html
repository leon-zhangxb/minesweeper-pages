Six Levels of Challenge

Beginner

Beginner — 9×9, 10 mines

The classic first step. Small board, few mines, perfect for learning the rules and building confidence.

Intermediate

Intermediate — 9×12, 20 mines

The original Windows 3.1 board, slightly taller. A gentle step up that sharpens pattern reading.

Expert

Expert — 9×16, 30 mines

The Windows 95 expert board. Demands careful flagging and a sharp eye for numeric patterns.

Ultra

Ultra — 16×16, 50 mines

A wider battlefield where every click matters. For players who want more room to plan.

Hell

Hell — 16×30, 99 mines

480 cells, 99 mines, one wrong move ends it all. The ultimate endurance test for veteran players.

Custom

Custom — your size, your mines

Pick any board from 9–36 columns by 9–64 rows, and any mine count from 1 up to cells − 1.

Why You’ll Love It

  • Authentic Windows 95 visual style — the gray chrome, the beveled cells, the smiley face, faithfully recreated.
  • Six difficulty levels + unlimited custom — from 9×9 beginner boards to 16×30 hellscapes.
  • First-tap safety — the mines are placed only after your first click, so you never lose on the opening move.
  • Long-press to flag, or toggle flag mode and tap to mark suspected mines. The mine counter updates live.
  • Chord tap — tap a number whose flagged neighbors equal its value to auto-reveal the rest. The classic desktop shortcut.
  • Hint button — when truly stuck, reveal one random safe cell and keep going.
  • Pause & resume automatically when the app goes to background, so your timer never cheats you.
  • Haptic feedback on flag, win, and loss, so the game feels alive in your hands.
  • Best-time tracking for every difficulty — chase your own records, not someone else’s.
  • 16 interface languages, switchable at any time and applied instantly.
  • 100% offline — no ads, no accounts, no analytics, no network of any kind. Just you and the board.
  • Portrait & landscape on iPhone and iPad.

More Than a Game

Minesweeper has been a fixture of personal computing since the early 1990s. Microsoft’s 1992 Windows 3.1 release made it a household name, and the 1995 Windows 95 version — with its smiley face, mine counter, and 16×16 expert board — is the one most people remember. Generations of office workers, students, and travelers have sharpened their logic on this grid.

This app keeps that 1995 feel intact on modern iOS: the same gray palette, the same first-click safety, the same chord-tap shortcut — rebuilt in SwiftUI for the iPhone and iPad you carry today.

经典扫雷 — 复刻 Windows 95 的怀旧体验

扫雷（Minesweeper）是 20 世纪 90 年代最广为人知的单人逻辑游戏之一。 1992 年随 Windows 3.1 进入大众视野，1995 年随 Windows 95 一同推出的版本—— 灰色边框、笑脸表情、地雷计数器与 16×16 的专家级棋盘——成为一代人共同的 电脑记忆。

本应用以 SwiftUI 完整复刻这份经典：6 种内置难度 + 无限自定义、长按插旗、 和弦点击、灯泡提示、首次点击安全、暂停恢复与触觉反馈；并提供 16 种界面语言、 完全离线、无广告无账号。让这款跨越三十年的经典在 iPhone 与 iPad 上焕发新生。
