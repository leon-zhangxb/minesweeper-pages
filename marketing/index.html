Six Classic Levels — Plus a Daily Challenge

Beginner

Beginner — 9×9, 10 mines

The classic first step. Small board, few mines, perfect for learning the rules and building confidence.

Intermediate

Intermediate — 9×12, 20 mines

A gentle step up that sharpens pattern reading without overwhelming you.

Expert

Expert — 9×16, 30 mines

Taller board, denser mines. Demands careful flagging and a sharp eye for numeric patterns.

Ultra

Ultra — 12×21, 50 mines

The largest standard board, shaped for portrait phones: bigger cells, more room to plan, every click matters.

Hell

Hell — 16×30, 99 mines

480 cells, 99 mines, one wrong move ends it all. The ultimate endurance test for veteran players.

Daily

Daily Challenge — 10×16, 33 mines

One board for the whole world, generated from the date. Same layout for everyone, resets at midnight — race the daily leaderboard and keep your streak alive.

Custom

Custom — your size, your mines

Pick any board from 9–36 columns by 9–64 rows, and any mine count from 1 up to cells − 1.

Why You’ll Love It

  • Three visual themes — iOS, Candy, and Neon. Drag the slider and the board re-skins live as you drag.
  • A new Daily Challenge every day — one shared board for all players, a daily leaderboard, and an “Unbroken Streak” achievement at 7, 30, and 100 days.

  • A little personality — the app talks to you: a line on cold launch, and a personal remark after every win or loss.

  • Portrait & landscape on iPhone and iPad.

More Than a Game

Minesweeper has been a fixture of personal computing since the early 1990s. Microsoft’s 1992 Windows 3.1 release made it a household name, and the 1995 Windows 95 version — with its smiley face, mine counter, and 99-mine expert board — is the one most people remember. Generations of office workers, students, and travelers have sharpened their logic on this grid.

This app keeps the 1995 rules intact on modern iOS: first-click safety, the same chord-tap shortcut, the same smiley reset — rebuilt in SwiftUI, with three visual themes so you can play it as classic, candy, or neon as you like.

扫雷（Minesweeper）是 20 世纪 90 年代最广为人知的单人逻辑游戏之一。 1992 年随 Windows 3.1 进入大众视野，1995 年随 Windows 95 一同推出的版本—— 笑脸表情、地雷计数器与 99 雷的专家级棋盘——成为一代人共同的电脑记忆。
