Privacy Policy

Minesweeper · Last updated: October 15, 2026

Minesweeper (the “App”) is developed by Wuhan Jindouzi Technology Co., Ltd (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

In short: the App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It works entirely offline, and everything it saves stays locally on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Usage analytics, diagnostics, or crash logs
  • Advertising identifiers (such as IDFA)
  • Account credentials — the App has no account system

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App saves the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your selected difficulty (Beginner, Intermediate, Expert, Ultra, Hell, or Custom)
  • Custom difficulty configuration (board size and mine count, when you use Custom)
  • Your best completion time for each difficulty level, so the App can show your personal record
  • Your interface language preference

This data is stored in iOS UserDefaults. It never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the App removes all of it.

3. Network Access

The App is fully functional offline and does not access the Internet at any time. There is no remote content, no leaderboard, no cloud sync, and no analytics upload. The App contains no external links.

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates no third-party services of any kind, including but not limited to: analytics (e.g., Firebase), advertising (e.g., AdMob), crash reporting, social login, AI services, and payment services. StoreKit and in-app purchases are not used.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). It is safe for use by children and families.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because the App collects no personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, delete, or export. Any data the App stores locally can be removed entirely by deleting the App.

7. Data Security

All App data is stored locally on your device and protected by iOS platform security. No data is transmitted to any server.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

隐私政策

经典扫雷（Minesweeper） · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 15 日

经典扫雷（下称“本应用”）由 Wuhan Jindouzi Technology Co., Ltd（下称“我们”）开发。 本隐私政策说明本应用如何处理您的信息。

简而言之：本应用不收集、不传输、不共享任何个人数据。 应用完全离线运行，所有保存的数据仅留在您的设备本地。

1. 我们不收集的信息

  • 个人身份信息（姓名、邮箱、电话号码等）
  • 位置数据
  • 通讯录、照片、相机或麦克风数据
  • 使用分析、诊断或崩溃日志
  • 广告标识符（如 IDFA）
  • 账号凭证——本应用没有账号系统

2. 仅存储于您设备上的信息

本应用仅在您的设备本地保存以下数据：

  • 您选择的难度（初级、中级、高级、顶级、地狱或自定义）
  • 自定义难度的配置（棋盘大小与地雷数，使用“自定义”时）
  • 各难度的最佳完成时间（便于应用显示您的个人记录）
  • 界面语言偏好

这些数据保存在 iOS UserDefaults 中，永远不会离开您的设备，我们也无法访问。 删除本应用即会同时删除这些数据。

3. 网络访问

本应用完全离线运行，任何时候都不会访问互联网。没有远程内容、没有排行榜、 没有云端同步、也没有分析上传。本应用不含任何外部链接。

4. 第三方服务

本应用未集成任何第三方服务，包括但不限于：数据分析（如 Firebase）、 广告（如 AdMob）、崩溃上报、社交登录、AI 服务与支付服务。本应用不使用 StoreKit，也不含内购。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用不向任何人（包括 13 周岁以下儿童）收集任何个人信息，适合儿童与家庭使用。

6. 您的权利（GDPR / CCPA）

由于本应用不收集任何个人数据，我们没有任何个人数据可供访问、更正、删除或导出。 应用本地存储的数据可通过删除本应用完全移除。

7. 数据安全

本应用的所有数据均存储在您设备本地，受 iOS 平台安全机制保护，不会传输至任何服务器。

8. 政策变更

我们可能不时更新本隐私政策。最新版本将始终发布在本页面，并同步更新“最后更新”日期。
