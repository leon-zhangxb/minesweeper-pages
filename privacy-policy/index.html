Privacy Policy

Minesweeper · Last updated: November 3, 2026

Minesweeper (the “App”) is developed by Wuhan Jindouzi Technology Co., Ltd (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

In short: the App itself collects no personal data. Apple Game Center is an optional feature — if you choose to sign in, your player nickname and game scores are handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we never receive that data. Without Game Center, the App works entirely offline.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We (the Developer) do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Usage analytics, diagnostics, or crash logs
  • Advertising identifiers (such as IDFA)
  • Account credentials — the App has no account system of its own

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App saves the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your selected difficulty (Beginner, Intermediate, Expert, Ultra, Hell, Daily Challenge, or Custom)
  • Custom difficulty configuration (board size and mine count, when you use Custom)
  • Your best completion time for each difficulty level, so the App can show your personal record
  • Daily Challenge records: completed days, current streak, and today’s best time
  • Achievement-reporting flags, so each achievement is submitted only once
  • Your theme and sound preferences
  • Your interface language preference (the App follows your system language)

This data is stored in iOS UserDefaults. It never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the App removes all of it.

3. Network Access

The App is fully playable offline. The only network activity is the optional Apple Game Center described in section 4. There is no remote content, no cloud sync, no analytics upload, and no third-party request of any kind.

4. Game Center (Optional)

Apple Game Center is Apple’s gaming service. Signing in is optional — the App is fully playable without it.

When you enable Game Center, the App submits your completion times to leaderboards and your achievement progress to Apple, and reads your leaderboard rank. This data is processed by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We operate no server of our own and never receive, store, or have access to your Game Center nickname, scores, or profile.

You can turn Game Center off at any time in iOS Settings → Game Center, and manage or clear your Game Center data through Apple.

5. Third-Party Services

The App includes no third-party SDKs of any kind, including but not limited to: analytics (e.g., Firebase), advertising (e.g., AdMob), crash reporting, social login, AI services, and payment services. StoreKit and in-app purchases are not used. Apple Game Center is a first-party platform service, described in section 4.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). It is safe for use by children and families.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we collect no personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, delete, or export. Any data the App stores locally can be removed entirely by deleting the App. Game Center data is managed by Apple — see section 4.

8. Data Security

All App data is stored locally on your device and protected by iOS platform security. No data is transmitted to any server operated by us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

隐私政策

经典扫雷（Minesweeper） · 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 3 日

经典扫雷（下称“本应用”）由 Wuhan Jindouzi Technology Co., Ltd（下称“我们”）开发。 本隐私政策说明本应用如何处理您的信息。

简而言之：本应用自身不收集任何个人数据。 Apple Game Center 为可选功能——若您选择登录，玩家昵称与游戏成绩由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理，我们不会获得这些数据；不启用 Game Center 时，本应用完全离线运行。

1. 我们不收集的信息

我们（开发者）不收集以下任何信息：

  • 个人身份信息（姓名、邮箱、电话号码等）
  • 位置数据
  • 通讯录、照片、相机或麦克风数据
  • 使用分析、诊断或崩溃日志
  • 广告标识符（如 IDFA）
  • 账号凭证——本应用自身没有账号系统

2. 仅存储于您设备上的信息

本应用仅在您的设备本地保存以下数据：

  • 您选择的难度（初级、中级、高级、顶级、地狱、每日挑战或自定义）
  • 自定义难度的配置（棋盘大小与地雷数，使用“自定义”时）
  • 各难度的最佳完成时间（便于应用显示您的个人记录）
  • 每日挑战记录：已完成日期、当前连续天数与当日最佳用时
  • 成就上报标记（确保每个成就仅上报一次）
  • 您的主题与音效偏好
  • 界面语言偏好（本应用跟随系统语言）

这些数据保存在 iOS UserDefaults 中，永远不会离开您的设备，我们也无法访问。 删除本应用即会同时删除这些数据。

3. 网络访问

本应用完全可离线游玩。唯一的联网行为是第 4 节所述的可选 Apple Game Center。 没有远程内容、没有云端同步、没有分析上传，也没有任何第三方网络请求。

4. Game Center（可选）

Apple Game Center 是 Apple 提供的游戏服务，登录为可选操作—— 不登录也可完整游玩本应用。

当您启用 Game Center 后，本应用会向排行榜提交您的通关用时、向 Apple 提交成就进度， 并读取您的排行榜名次。上述数据由 Apple 依据 Apple 隐私政策处理。 我们不运营任何自有服务器，也不会获得、存储或访问您的 Game Center 昵称、成绩或资料。

您可以随时在 iOS 设置 → Game Center 中关闭该功能，并通过 Apple 管理或清除 您的 Game Center 数据。

5. 第三方服务

本应用未集成任何第三方 SDK，包括但不限于：数据分析（如 Firebase）、 广告（如 AdMob）、崩溃上报、社交登录、AI 服务与支付服务。本应用不使用 StoreKit，也不含内购。 Apple Game Center 属于 Apple 一方平台服务，详见第 4 节。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不向任何人（包括 13 周岁以下儿童）收集任何个人信息，适合儿童与家庭使用。

7. 您的权利（GDPR / CCPA）

由于我们不收集任何个人数据，我们没有任何个人数据可供访问、更正、删除或导出。 应用本地存储的数据可通过删除本应用完全移除。Game Center 数据由 Apple 管理，见第 4 节。

8. 数据安全

本应用的所有数据均存储在您设备本地，受 iOS 平台安全机制保护，不会传输至我们运营的任何服务器。

9. 政策变更

我们可能不时更新本隐私政策。最新版本将始终发布在本页面，并同步更新“最后更新”日期。
